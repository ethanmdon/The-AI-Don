Credit Card Spending Analyzer

Copy this prompt. Paste it into Claude with your transaction data. Get your full financial breakdown in seconds.

AI Prompt — Claude.ai
You are an expert personal finance analyst. I'm going to give you my credit card transaction data for the past month (or more). Your job is to analyze my spending and give me a CLEAR, HONEST, and ACTIONABLE financial breakdown. Here's what I need you to do: ───────────────────────────────────────── STEP 1 — CATEGORIZE ALL SPENDING ───────────────────────────────────────── Group every transaction into categories such as: • Food & Dining (restaurants, delivery, groceries) • Entertainment & Streaming • Shopping & Apparel • Health & Wellness • Transportation • Subscriptions & Memberships • Travel • Utilities & Bills • Other / Miscellaneous Show a clean table: | Category | Total Spent | # of Transactions | % of Total | ───────────────────────────────────────── STEP 2 — SUBSCRIPTIONS AUDIT ───────────────────────────────────────── Find ALL recurring charges — monthly or annual. List them in a table: | Subscription | Amount | Frequency | Monthly Cost | Then tell me: • Total monthly subscription spend • Which ones look unused or redundant • Which ones I should consider canceling ───────────────────────────────────────── STEP 3 — SPENDING TRENDS ───────────────────────────────────────── If data spans multiple months: • Show spending by month • Identify which categories increased or decreased • Flag any unusual spikes ───────────────────────────────────────── STEP 4 — TOP MERCHANTS ───────────────────────────────────────── List my Top 10 merchants by total spend. Show: | Merchant | Total | # Visits | Avg Per Visit | ───────────────────────────────────────── STEP 5 — SAVINGS OPPORTUNITIES ───────────────────────────────────────── Based on what you see, give me 5–7 SPECIFIC and REALISTIC ways to reduce spending. Not generic advice. Tell me exactly: • Which category to cut • By how much • How much I'd save per month ───────────────────────────────────────── STEP 6 — FINANCIAL HEALTH SCORE ───────────────────────────────────────── Give me a spending health score from 1–100. Break it down by: • Subscription load • Dining vs. grocery balance • Discretionary vs. essential ratio • Spending consistency ───────────────────────────────────────── FINAL SUMMARY ───────────────────────────────────────── End with a short 3–5 bullet "What to do this week" action plan. Make it simple. Make it specific. Make it doable. ───────────────────────────────────────── HERE IS MY TRANSACTION DATA: ───────────────────────────────────────── [PASTE YOUR TRANSACTIONS HERE — CSV, copy/paste from bank, or typed list]
